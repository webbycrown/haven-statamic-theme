Oxford is a basket weave. It creases, and that is the cloth. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

A crisp morning shirt is the wrong expectation. The weave is why it feels soft and why it will not stay pressed. The collar rubs the line first. Inside out slows that, and it keeps the buttons from catching other cloth.
