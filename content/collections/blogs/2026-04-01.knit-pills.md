Pills are loose fibre, not a thin cloth. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

The underarm and the side where a strap sits go first. A comb lifts the balls. Do not pull them. A hanger pulls the neck out of shape. A shelf, folded once, keeps the shoulder where it was cut.
