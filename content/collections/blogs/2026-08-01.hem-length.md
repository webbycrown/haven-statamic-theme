A hem is a measurement from the floor, not from a photograph. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

A hem that looks right barefoot is short in a boot and long in a flat. Send the shoe height with the question. Check both. A cloth that hangs longer at the back is the cut, or it is the posture. Look before you ask for a change.
