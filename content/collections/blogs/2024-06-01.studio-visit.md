The studio is a working room, and a visit is a short hour in it, not a tour of every rail.

Three lengths, the note card for each, and the sample that has already been washed are enough. You can see why a weight stayed. You can feel the difference between the cloth as it arrived and the cloth after a wash. That difference is the reason some hems are cut long.

Come with one question. A size, a cloth, or a piece you have already seen on the site. The hour is more useful when it has a point.
