A studio visit is a short hour at the table where the current cloth is laid out.

We do not walk people through every rail. Three lengths, the note card for each, and the sample that has already been washed are enough to understand how the edit is chosen. If a question is about a size, it is better asked in writing so the answer can include a measurement.

The room is a working room. Cutting happens in the morning. Visits sit in the afternoon, when the table is clear and the light is still honest.
