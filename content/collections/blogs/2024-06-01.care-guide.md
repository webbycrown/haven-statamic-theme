Basics first, because most damage is heat and delay.

Wash only when the piece needs it. Air a wool. Shake a linen. A cotton trouser can take a short gentle cycle and should be pulled into shape while it is damp, then dried away from a radiator. The dryer is the step we would remove from most households if we could.

Read the line sewn into the piece before you read a general guide. That line was written for the cloth in your hand.
