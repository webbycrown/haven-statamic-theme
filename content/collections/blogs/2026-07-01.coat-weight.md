Choose the weight for the weather you actually stand in. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

Mid-weight wool is the one that gets worn. A heavy coat stays on the hook. If the lining pulls at the hem when you lift an arm, the coat will twist. That is a fit note, not a style.
