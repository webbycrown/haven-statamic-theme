Evening oil is the last thing on the face, and it should stay the last thing.

We pour a light oil because a heavy one pillows on the skin and transfers to the pillow. The point is a quiet sheen you do not notice in the morning, not a film you can see. If the face looks wet after a minute, you used too much. Blot once. Do not add powder on top to hide it.

Scent is faint on purpose. A strong perfume oil is a different product, and it fights whatever you already wear. This one should disappear into the room.
