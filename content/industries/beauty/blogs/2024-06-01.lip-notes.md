Lip colour in this edit is a stain with a little slip, not a paint.

The useful test is a Tuesday. If the colour looks like costume when you are only going to the shop, it is too much, however well it photographed. A red that reads as your own mouth, a little clearer, is the one that leaves the studio.

Apply from the bullet or with a finger. Brushes make a sharper edge than this formula wants. Press, do not draw. The centre of the lip can stay sheerer than the edge.
