Hands dry out from water, soap, and paper, in that order.

The cream in this edit is thick enough to stay in a tin and light enough to vanish before you pick up a pen. We skipped a pump bottle because a pump doses too much and then the rest of the bottle sits in a humid room and separates.

Use it after the sink, not on a schedule. Morning and the end of the day are enough if the tin is actually there. A third time belongs to a day of washing-up or a day in the cold.
