Linen looks right after it has been washed, not before. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

A cloth that only looks smooth before the first laundry will crease in a day and stay that way. Cool water and a low dry, or a line. A hot iron on a damp seam is how linen shines in the wrong place.
