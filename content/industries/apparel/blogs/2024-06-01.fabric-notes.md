Fabric notes start on the table, with the cloth in the hand, before a pattern is cut.

We look for a weight you can wear for a full day without it clinging or standing away from the body. Linen that is too fine wrinkles into strings. Wool that is too thick belongs in a coat, not a shirt. The middle weights are the ones that earn a place in the edit.

Colour is checked in daylight and again under a lamp. A blue that looks clean at noon can turn grey at a desk. If it fails the lamp, it does not go on the rail.
