Fade is the cloth relaxing, not a fault in the dye. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

Turn the jacket if you want the fade slower. Expect the seams to stay darker. That line is the make. Air it. Spot the cuffs. A full wash every wear is how a jacket goes pale before the season ends.
