The care guide is the same card we wrote when the cloth was chosen. It is not a general laundry sermon.

Read the line on the piece. Linen in this edit wants a wash and then a shake, not an ironing session. Wool wants air and a brush, and almost never a machine. A cotton trouser can take a gentle wash and should be dried so the leg stays a leg.

Heat is the step that goes wrong. It shines a wool, shrinks a linen past the hem we cut, and flattens a texture on purpose.
