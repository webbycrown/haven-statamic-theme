side_copy: |
  Recut the ends when you change the water. A stem seals itself. A fresh cut is what drinks. Warm water for woody stems, cool water for soft ones. Nothing else in the vase. Sugar and coins are folklore. Clean water is the method.

  Keep the arrangement out of a ripe fruit bowl. Fruit hurries the drop.

  One kind of stem is enough. Mixed bouquets fight. A branch, or a single flower repeated, reads as chosen.
closing: |
  When the stems are done, compost them and wash the vase before it smells. A dirty vase is why the next bunch fails on day one.

  Flowers are a few days. That is the agreement. They are not a houseplant, and they should not be asked to act like one.

Stems are for the table you eat at, or the sill you pass.

We send branches and a few cut flowers that hold in a cool room. A hot hallway shortens everything. If you can, give them the night in a sink of water before they go in the vase. That drink is the difference between a branch that opens and a branch that stays shut.

The arrangement should sit below eye level when you are seated. A tall stem between two people is a wall. Move it to the end of the table.
