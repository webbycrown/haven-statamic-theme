Dried stems are the plant for a room that is often empty.

We keep the bunches small so they do not dominate a shelf. One bunch in a narrow vase is the edit. A second bunch belongs in another room, or it starts to look like a store.

The colour is the colour of the dried plant, not a paint. If you want a brighter note, that is a fresh stem for the week, not a dyed grass. We do not sell dyed bunches for that reason.
