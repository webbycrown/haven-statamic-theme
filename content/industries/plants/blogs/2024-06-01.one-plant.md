side_copy: |
  One pot is a complete edit. A second plant is for a second room. A shelf of pots looks like a project and dies like one, because nobody can see which one is thirsty.

  Dust the leaves when you water. A grey film is not the plant failing. It is the room.

  Repot only when roots are circling and the water runs straight through. A larger pot too soon keeps the soil wet. Most plants in this edit are happier slightly held in.

One plant is a decision about the room, not a collection.

We choose plants that forgive a missed week and a north-ish window, because a rare one that needs a greenhouse will not survive a desk. The pot is part of the object. A speckled clay pot that matches the vase on the same sill is the point. A plastic grower pot can stay inside it. You do not have to show the nursery pot, and you do not have to disturb the roots to hide it.

Turn the pot a quarter turn when you water, so the plant does not lean permanently toward the glass.
