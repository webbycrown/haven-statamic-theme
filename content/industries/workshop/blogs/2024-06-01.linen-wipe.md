  Keep two piles. Dry linen for dust before a finish. Oily linen for wiping a coat on, then off. Mixing them is how dust gets into the oil and the surface goes grey.

A linen wipe is the cloth between the finish and the wood.

Paper tears, leaves lint in a wet coat, and cannot take a second pass of oil without falling apart. Linen holds together when it is damp with oil, and it does not shed into the grain. That is why the roll is in the workshop edit and not with the home throws.

Cut from the roll. Do not tear. A torn edge frays into the finish. A hem is unnecessary. A clean cut is enough.
