The desk is a small landscape, and most of it should be boring.

We keep the stationery edit to a notebook, an ink, envelopes, and a seal because those four cover a letter and a day of notes. Anything else on the desk is your work, not the shop's. A pen cup with twelve pens is how you spend the first minute choosing. One pen that is filled is the better object.

At the end of the day, close the book, cap the ink, and leave the rectangle clear. Tomorrow starts faster.
