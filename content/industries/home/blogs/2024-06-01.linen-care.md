Linen care is a wash, a dry, and a refusal to fight the crease.

The cloth wrinkles because of what it is. Pressing it into a board-flat sheet makes it look nervous and lasts until you sit down. Shake it out, smooth the worst of the fold, and use it. A table cloth in particular wants to look as if a meal could happen, not as if a presser just left.

Wash when it is actually marked. Linen can air between uses. Soap should be gentle, and the spin should not wring the life out of a hem.
