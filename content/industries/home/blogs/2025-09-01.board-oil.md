Oil a board when water stops beading. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

Food-safe oil, thin, both faces and the edge. Stand it to dry. A thick coat is sticky and wrong. A board in a full sink swells and cracks later. Wipe it. Wash it quickly if you must. Dry it at once.
