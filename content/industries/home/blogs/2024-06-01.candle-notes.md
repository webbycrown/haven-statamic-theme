  The vessel is part of the burn. A glass that is too narrow tunnels. A wide one in a small room is more scent than you asked for. We pour for a table or a shelf, and the note on the piece says which.

  Never leave a flame in an empty room. That is not a style rule. Wax and wick are a fire if you forget them.

Candle notes are about the burn, not about collecting tins.

Place the candle on a surface that can take heat, away from a curtain and a draught. A draught makes the flame lean and blacks the glass. Trim the wick to a short, even tip before every light. A long wick smokes, and the smoke is what stains a ceiling over a season.

Let the wax melt level. Then put it out by dipping the wick into the pool and straightening it, or with a snuffer. Blowing hard throws soot and wax.
