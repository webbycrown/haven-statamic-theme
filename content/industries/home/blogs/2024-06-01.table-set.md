A table set is a small list, repeated until it feels inevitable.

Start with the surface. If the table is wood you care about, the cloth or the mat is not decoration. It is the layer that takes the heat and the spill. Then the plate or the bowl you use most nights, not the set kept for guests. Guests can eat from the same bowl. The evening is calmer when you are not swapping cupboards.

Light sits above or to the side, warm enough to see the food and low enough that nobody squints. One source is usually enough.
