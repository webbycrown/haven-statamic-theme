A handle should take two fingers without the knuckle hitting the cup. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

Pick it up full. The balance changes. A handle that felt fine empty can twist when the mug is full. A thick rim changes the way it drinks. That is a preference. A rough rim is not.
