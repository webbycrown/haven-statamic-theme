A glaze should feel even under the thumb, inside and out. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

A sharp spot on the rim is a flaw, not a charm. The inside should be as finished as the face you see. Fine lines in the glaze can be the material. A chip is not. Send a photograph if you are unsure.
