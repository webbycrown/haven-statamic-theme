The evening bulb is the one you live with after the day has ended.

We spec a warm tone in the desk lamp because the lamp sits close to the face. A cool bulb at that distance is tiring in a way people blame on the chair. Change the bulb before you move the furniture.

The rest of the room can stay dimmer than the page. You need to see the book. You do not need to see every corner.
