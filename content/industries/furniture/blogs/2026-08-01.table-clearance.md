Leave a path around the table, not only room for the chairs. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

A table that fills the room is a table you cannot use. Measure the path with a chair pulled out, not pushed in. A thick apron under the top lowers the space for knees. Ask for that measurement if the top height looks right but the seat feels tight.
