A stool is for a short sit, and the height should say so. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

A stool at a table leaves your arms too high. A stool at a counter that is too low leaves you hunched. Measure the counter first. If your feet dangle, you will not sit there. The rest is part of the height.
