Elbows should rest with the shoulders down. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

Sit in the chair you will use. The desk height follows the chair, not a standard from a catalogue. Roll the chair in. If the arms hit the top, one of them is wrong. Usually it is the desk apron.
