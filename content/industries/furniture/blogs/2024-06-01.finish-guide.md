The finish guide is short because the wrong habit is usually the same one: too much product, too often.

Dust with a barely damp cloth and dry it. Household polishes build a film that looks smeared and is hard to remove. If the piece is oiled, a drop of the same oil once or twice a year, rubbed thin, is the maintenance. If it is a harder film, do not oil it at all.

Heat and water are the pair to respect. A pan, a laptop that runs hot, and a glass with a wet foot will all print themselves on a young finish.
