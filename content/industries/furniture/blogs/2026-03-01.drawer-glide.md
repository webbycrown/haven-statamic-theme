A drawer should open with one hand and stop before it leaves the carcase. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

Tell us what you plan to keep in it. Cutlery and a laptop are different weights. An unfinished drawer marks what you store. The same oil as the outside, on the parts your hands touch.
