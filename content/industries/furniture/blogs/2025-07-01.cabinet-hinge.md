A door should close by itself the last few centimetres, or stay where you leave it. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

Tell us if the floor is uneven. A cabinet that was square in the workshop can twist on a sloping floor. A fixed shelf is final. Pin holes let the shelf move when the objects change.
