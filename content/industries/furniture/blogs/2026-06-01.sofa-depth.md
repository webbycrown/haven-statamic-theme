Depth is how far back the seat goes, and whether you can stand up. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

If your feet leave the floor, the sofa is too deep for the way you sit. That is a measurement, not a taste. A cushion that creeps forward every evening is a fill problem. We would rather know that in the first week.
