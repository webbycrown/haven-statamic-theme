Measure the path through the room before the piece. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

A sofa that blocks the door is the wrong size, even if it fits the wall. Walk the path with a tape. A table that turns the corner on paper may not turn it in the stair. Send the stair width if the piece is large.
