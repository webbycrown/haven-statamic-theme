Oil is a finish you renew, not a coat you forget. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

Wipe with the same oil, thin, along the grain. A thick coat stays sticky. Once a season is enough in a dry room. If a drop soaks in, the finish is thin. Oil it before the mark becomes a ring.
