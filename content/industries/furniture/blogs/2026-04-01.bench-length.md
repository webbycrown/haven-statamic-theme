A bench should stop short of the table ends. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

Leave room to walk around. Two people need less length than three, and the table sets that number. People stand up from a bench by leaning. A heavy bench that will not shift a little is awkward. A fixed one belongs against a wall.
