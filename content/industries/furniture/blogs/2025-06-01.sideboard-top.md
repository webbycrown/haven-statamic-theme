The top should take a platter without a mat, if that is how you live. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

We oil the top for spills you wipe at once. A flood left overnight is still a mark. The note is: wipe it. Measure the piece you actually own. A sideboard that is pretty and too low becomes a shelf you cannot use.
