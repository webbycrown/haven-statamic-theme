Decide if it is a footrest, a seat, or a table. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

A soft top is a footrest. A firm top can take a tray. A seat needs a height that matches the sofa. An ottoman pushed into the only path will be moved every day, badly. Give it a place.
