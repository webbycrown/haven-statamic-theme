Room notes are about the floor you walk, not about filling a wall.

Start with the path. Where do you enter, where do you sit, where is the light. A table that blocks the path will be hated in a week, even if the top is the right oak. We would rather a smaller top and a clear walk than a generous piece you have to squeeze past.

Height is the next note. A desk that is too tall lifts the shoulders. A sofa that is too deep swallows a person who wants to read. The edit is scaled for rooms people sit in, not for a showroom with a higher ceiling than yours.
