A stone that moves in the setting is not a cleaning job. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

If you can shift the stone with a fingernail, stop wearing it. Send a photograph with the inquiry. A bezel hides the edge. A claw shows it, and a claw is what catches a sleeve. Check claws when you check the clasp.
