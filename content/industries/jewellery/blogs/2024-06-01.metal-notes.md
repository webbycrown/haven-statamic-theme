Choosing a metal for daily wear is mostly a question of finish, not of how bright it looks in a photograph.

A softer gold tone sits closer to skin and does not flash under indoor light. It also forgives the small scuffs that come from a bag strap, a keyboard, and a coat cuff. We edit the jewellery collection around that kind of wear, because a piece that only looks right in the box does not earn its place.

Weight matters as much as colour. A fine chain should be light enough to forget, and a band should be smooth on the inside edge so it does not catch on fabric. If a piece feels sharp in the hand, it will feel sharp on the wrist.
