Measure the finger you will wear it on, at the end of the day. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

Use a strip of paper, mark the overlap, and send the length with the inquiry. A guess from another ring is a start, not a size. The same number can feel tight on a signet and loose on a wire band. Say which piece the size is for.
