A post should sit straight and close without a pinch. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

If the back slides free when you tilt your head, it is the wrong tightness. Send a note before you lose the second one. A heavy drop on a thin post pulls. If the ear is sore after an hour, the piece is too heavy for that post.
