One soft compartment per piece is the whole packing rule. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

Coil a chain and pin it, or use a pouch. A loose chain in a wash bag comes home as a knot. Travel is for wearing, not for cleaning. A cloth is enough. Paste and a hotel sink are how a finish gets cloudy.
