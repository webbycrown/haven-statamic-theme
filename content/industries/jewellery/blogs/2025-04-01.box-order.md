The way a box is packed is the way the pieces survive. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

A pearl under a bangle is a scratch. A chain loose on top is a knot. A name on the pouch saves a morning of sorting. It also stops the wrong clasp going into the wrong compartment.
