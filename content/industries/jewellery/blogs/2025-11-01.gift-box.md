The box is for handing over, not for storage. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

Move the piece to a soft pouch after it is opened. The box hinge and the card are what people keep. The piece wants cloth. A short line about water and perfume is more useful than a long card. The person who receives it will not hunt for a booklet.
