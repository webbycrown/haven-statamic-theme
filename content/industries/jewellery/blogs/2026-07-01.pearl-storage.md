Pearls want a cloth, not a jumble of clasps. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

A strand crushed in a box develops a kink you cannot shake out. The silk is the part that fails first. A ring or a chain in the same compartment will mark the surface. A soft pouch is enough.
