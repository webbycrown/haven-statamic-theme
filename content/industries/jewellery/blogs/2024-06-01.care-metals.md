Care for a daily piece is mostly storage and a cloth, not a ritual.

Take jewellery off before sleep, sport, and the sink. That single habit prevents more damage than any polish. Chains tangle in a pocket. Rings knock a stone when they meet a counter. Studs are happier in their box than loose in a bag.

When you do clean, use the cloth that came with the piece, or any soft cotton that will not snag. Wipe the front, the back, and the clasp. If the piece was in the sea or a pool, rinse with plain water first, then dry it before it goes away.
