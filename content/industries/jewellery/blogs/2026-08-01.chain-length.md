Length is a measurement, not a style name. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

Ask for the length in centimetres if you are sending an inquiry. Choker, princess, and opera are not sizes. A clasp that rests at the back of the neck is comfortable. One that slides to the front will sit on the bone.
