A gift from this edit should leave the studio the same day and still feel considered when it is opened.

We start with pieces that do not depend on a precise fit. Ear studs, a fine chain, and a short pearl strand pack flat, sit well on most people, and do not ask the recipient to book a sizing. A ring is the exception. If you know the size, say so in the inquiry. If you do not, choose a chain.

The box is part of the gift. Keep it small, unprinted on the inside, and strong enough for a pocket. Tissue is enough. A large case makes a small piece feel like an apology.
