A clasp that hesitates is already a repair. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

A hinge that sticks, or a lobster that needs two hands, will fail on a day out. Test it at the table, not at the door. A post bends at the back. A toggle stretches the ring it passes through. Look at the part that moves, not only the part you see.
