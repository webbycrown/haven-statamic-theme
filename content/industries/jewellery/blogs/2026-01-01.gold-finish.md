A warm tone changes when it meets cream and perfume. The rest of the note is how we do that in the studio.

Perfume on a warm chain dulls the high points first. The cloth will not bring that colour back in one wipe. A yellow band and a paler chain can sit together. Match them only if you want one note. The edit does not require it.
